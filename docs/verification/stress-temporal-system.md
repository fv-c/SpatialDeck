# Stress test of SpatialDeck 0.6 with temporal-system

28–29 October 2026, on commit `6d0a09a` (0.6 + dark interface), with dists rebuilt on 27 October. A real, intensive deck about [temporal-system](../../../temporal-system) (same author) used as a test bench: every SpatialDeck capability is exercised at least once, many at the schema limits. The deck, the generators and the harnesses live in `examples/stress-temporal-system/`, only in the maintainer's local copy and excluded from the repository; the evidence is in `test-results/stress-temporal-system/` (ignored). The stress test did not modify the package sources; eleven defects were then fixed on 29 October (section «Fixes»). The deck is in Italian: titles and texts quoted from it below are kept as they are, and messages quoted from Studio are the Italian interface of the time.

Environment: macOS 14 (Darwin 23.6), Apple M3 Max, Node 24.22.0, pnpm 12.14.0, Playwright 1.64.1 (Chromium 156, Firefox) and the macOS 14 WebKit runner (1.61.1), FFmpeg 8.1, xpdf 4.05. The browser measurements are **headless**: Chromium and Firefox render in software, so frame times are pessimistic; the measurement with a real GPU in the browser pane is not valid because the pane was hidden (background page, frozen player) and remains **not run**.

Regeneration: `python3 -I examples/stress-temporal-system/make-assets.py ../temporal-system`, then `node examples/stress-temporal-system/generate.mjs` (validates with the core, writes `project.json`, `project-main.json`, three itineraries and the variants in `variants/`) and `node examples/stress-temporal-system/pack.mjs <json> <archive>`. The harnesses (`harness/*.mjs`) use the production build of Studio served by the `studio-dist` configuration of `.claude/launch.json`.

## What holds up well

- **Validation and opening.** `validate` takes 0.12 s for the deck (21 ms of core), 0.31 s for 1000 scenes; production Studio opens the 3.8 MB archive in 0.2–0.35 s.
- **Navigation.** The full itinerary (56 visits: branches, rejoins, a loop repeated twice, a manual gate) runs identically in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit; visit limits and reasons for disabling are correct; the text view exposes all 48 stops.
- **Security.** Literal text with `</script>`, `javascript:` in Markdown and KaTeX, handlers, `style`, `iframe`, `foreignObject`, remote `href` and recursive macros stay inert; no private note in the public DOM or in the exports.
- **Layout.** Dragging 1 or 100 blocks together: pointermove → two frames 74 ms p50 / 85 ms p95 (headless), a single command per gesture, undo in 50 ms. The diagnostics find contrast (1.13:1), clipped text and a frame over a link.
- **Persistence.** Autosave in IndexedDB (2.2 → 5.8 MB), Save archive 161 ms (3.8 MB), Save JSON 65 ms, the recovery offer 101 ms after reload, restore in 100 ms.
- **Export.** Standalone HTML in 0.2 s (8.5 MB, hash-based CSP); a strict 56-page PDF in 12.3 s (20 MiB, with `project-main.json`); a 41 s WebM at 1920×1080 in 225 s (1230 frames, duration and poses verified by the manifest, relative error 4.9e-6).

## Gaps and needs

**Time and animation inside the scene.** For a temporal subject, local time is exactly what is missing: no progressive builds of blocks, no cues with a visible or audible effect (the deck's 47 cues are only events for a host; no builtin renderer uses them), no sync between audio and camera tracks (the accelerando and the 120 BPM click cannot play together with the camera). Today the workaround is duplicating nodes or using camera moves.

**Camera authoring.** Everything is numeric: «Camera indipendente» (Independent camera) copies the scene framing and «Aggiungi keyframe» (Add keyframe) duplicates an existing pose (`CameraTimeline.tsx:142-149`, `:327-350`); the current view after pan or zoom cannot be captured. The 200-keyframe track becomes a list of 1600 fields. A visual timeline or curve and a graph view of the paths are missing (52 connections in a list, visits of the same scene indistinguishable: «beatToSeconds…» appears twice).

**Typography and style.** Sizes are relative to the body with a maximum `xxl` = 2.2×: titles readable on large sections only with SVG; a single title size in the theme; no per-block font (monospace code only inside Markdown, without highlighting); no per-block background, border or padding (every piano-roll note is an SVG). In flow scenes images are limited to 320 px of height (`packages/player/src/style.css:86`): the diagram of «L'idea generale» stays tiny in a 1600×900 scene.

**Sanitization too strict for diagrams.** SVG without `font-weight`, `font-family`, `stroke-dasharray`, `marker`, `fill-opacity`, `preserveAspectRatio`, `dominant-baseline` (`packages/plugins/src/security.ts:75-114`); HTML without cell alignment and column widths: the losses table wraps inside words («Featur e»).

**Structure.** No native groups, templates or «sections»: the billboard parent used as a substitute causes D7. The scene list is flat even with 11 hierarchy levels; the map is short and has no «fit to world» (2% to see everything).

**Distribution.** The CLI does not create `.spatialdeck` archives (Studio or `pack.mjs` is needed), registers no plugins (not even the badge declared `offline:true`) and does not export a subset. The exported HTML has no presenter tools (D6) and the presenter view does not work from `file://`. System fonts (`system-serif`) make PDF and video differ from one machine to another (here Times and Georgia). One minute of 1080p video takes about 5.5 minutes of capture.

**Performance (headless, to confirm with a GPU).** A 12 s spiral into the recursion: Chromium 50 ms p50 / 100 ms p95 per frame, Firefox 109/175 ms, WebKit 17/19 ms; zooming out on the section has 533 ms spikes. The 34 ms p95 budget cannot be verified without a GPU.

## Suggested priorities for 1.0

1. D1, D4, D5: wrong data or broken exports with ordinary content (fixed on 29 October, like D3, D9, D11–D13, D15–D17).
2. D2, D3, D6: they make exports and the HTML usable for a real presenter.
3. D7 and D8: fidelity across engines and a searchable PDF.
4. Authoring needs (capturing the view, builds, cues with effects) to be planned beyond 1.0 if the scope stays closed.
